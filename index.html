How it works

Four steps, and your staff do none of them.

  1. A patient messages

    On WhatsApp or Instagram, at any hour.

  2. AI replies

    Fees, timings and location, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

  3. The slot is booked

    The patient picks a free time without calling.

  4. You get the details

    Name, number, reason for the visit, and how serious the enquiry is.

Questions clinics ask

Do we need new software or a new number?

You keep your Instagram page. WhatsApp automation needs the official WhatsApp Business API, which uses a number that is not on the ordinary WhatsApp app. We set that up with you.

Will the AI give medical advice?

No. It answers practical questions such as fees, timings, location and booking, and passes anything medical to your staff.

What does it cost?

It depends on the clinic and the channels you use. We explain it on the demo call, and the 14-day trial is free.

Which clinics have you worked with?

We are new and say so. That is why the trial comes first and the fee comes later.

What happens to patient data?

We collect only what is needed to book the visit, message only people who contacted you, and stop when someone asks.
